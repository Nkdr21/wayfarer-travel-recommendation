About Us

Wayfarer is an independent travel discovery project. We help curious travelers choose destinations by pairing clear descriptions with illustrated guides to beaches, temples, and countries.

How our team works

Destination research
Verify each location’s name and country before adding it to the guide.
Editorial
Write a short description of what makes the destination distinctive. Label illustrations clearly.
Quality review
Test singular and plural searches, check that each result has an image and useful alternative text, and verify navigation on mobile.
